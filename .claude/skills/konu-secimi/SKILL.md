# Topic selection

1. Read `arastirma/video-havuzu.md`, `kanal/ogrenme-kaydi.md`, titles of existing `videolar/*/senaryo.json`.
2. Shortlist 5 candidates (mix of pillars). **Gate first — proven demand:** with `viral-tarama` on competitor channels (NextGen Manufacturing @NextGenManufacturings, The Factoran @TheFactoran, The B1M, MegaBuilds @megabuilds-us, Mustard, Real Engineering) and YouTube search, find ≥2 videos on the same or a very close subject, from ≥2 channels, in the last 24 months, each ≥1M views or ≥3x its channel median. No proof → drop the candidate (keep it in the pool as "idea"). Write the proof (title, channel, views, date) into the pool's Notes column. Then for each survivor check:
   - **Demand:** YouTube autocomplete (`anahtar-kelime`) and whether similar videos got views (outliers from `viral-tarama` if time allows).
   - **Researchability:** public numbers exist (project owners' and government reports, budgets, construction press such as ENR / New Civil Engineer, shipyard and port authority releases, credible press). Future projects need an official status source (approved, under construction, target year).
   - **Visuals:** stock footage plausible (construction, cranes, ships, ports, tunnels, factories) + NASA/Openverse images + at least one chart/cost tower. No copyrighted developer renders.
   - **Freshness:** news hook or evergreen.
   - **Safety:** not investment/health/political advice; no single private person as subject.
3. Score each 1-5 on Demand, Curiosity, Researchability, Visuals, Fit with learning log. Pick the top one; alternate pillars vs the last video. In the first 12 videos keep the mix ≈ 5 How It Was Built / 4 Giant Ships & Machines / 3 Building the Future and favour the subjects with the strongest proof.
4. Output: pillar, working title, one-sentence promise, 3 questions to answer, hook formula idea. Update the pool (mark used, add 3 fresh ideas).
