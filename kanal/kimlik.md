# Kanal Kimliği — Colossal Builds

> Ajanların başvuru dosyası. Kullanıcının okuması gerekmez. Karar: 19 Ekim 2026 (100 niş değerlendirmesi sonucu; önceki "Below The Waterline" denizcilik planı bu kanalın denizcilik ağırlığı olarak devam ediyor).

- **Name:** Colossal Builds · handle önerisi `@colossalbuilds` (yedekler: `@colossal.builds`, `@colossalbuildshq`)
- **Tagline:** How the biggest things on Earth are built, and what they really cost.
- **Promise:** Every video explains one colossal build — how it was made, the engineering trick that made it possible, and the bill: what it cost and who paid.
- **Audience:** Men 25-60 first (mega-builds audiences skew 40-60), US first, then UK / CA / AU. Engineers and tradespeople, boat and machinery enthusiasts, curious professionals. They watch The B1M, NextGen Manufacturing, The Factoran, Mustard, Real Engineering.
- **Pillars:**
  1. **How It Was Built** — completed megaprojects, maritime-heavy: canals, ports, sea bridges and tunnels, airports and islands built on the sea, dams, record buildings.
  2. **Giant Ships & Machines** — how giant ships, superyachts, offshore rigs and heavy machines are made and work; inside shipyards and factories. Owner's core expertise (10+ years sourcing every material group for 10-80 m yachts).
  3. **Building the Future** — REAL projects under construction or officially approved with target dates 2027-2050 (e.g. maglev lines, undersea tunnels, ship tunnels, new ports, floating-city pilots, NEOM). Never pure fantasy "cities of 2050": every future video is anchored to a named, sourced project and says clearly what is planned vs built.
- **Mix:** first 12 videos ≈ 5 How It Was Built, 4 Giant Ships & Machines, 3 Building the Future; later follow what the analytics say.
- **Edge (originality signal):** every video has (a) a **Cost Tower** of what the build cost and where the money went, and (b) when relevant a **"from the buyer's desk"** note: how materials, equipment and suppliers for a build like this are sourced, priced and delayed (general industry practice, never a private client). The owner reviews the key numbers before production.

## Proven-demand rule (no guessing)
A topic is produced only if similar videos already proved demand: at least 2 videos on the same or a very close subject, from 2 different channels, in the last 24 months, each ≥ 1M views or ≥ 3x its channel median (`viral-tarama`). Otherwise it stays in the pool. This lowers — it cannot remove — the risk of a video not being watched.

## Voice (narration)
- Calm, confident, awe without hype. Short sentences. Speaks to "you".
- Numbers concrete and sourced; costs in today's dollars where possible ("about 9 billion dollars").
- Future projects: always "planned", "proposed", "expected by 2035", never presented as finished.
- No "insane/shocking". No "Hey guys". No politics beyond facts needed for the story.
- Voice: Kokoro `am_michael` in the free test phase; ElevenLabs later.

## Visual identity
- Colors: deep navy #0B1220, accent amber #FFB703, white text, profit green #2BB673, cost red #E63946, grey #8A96A8.
- Fonts: Anton (headlines, thumbnails), Montserrat (charts, on-screen text). Both in `kanal/fontlar/`.
- Signature visuals: Cost Tower, amber price tag on thumbnails, NASA satellite before/after, simple map and timeline graphics.
- Thumbnails: one colossal subject (ship hull, bridge, crane, dam, tunnel boring machine), max 4 words, one amber word, price tag optional ("$9B"). No faces, no developer logos.
- Sources: Pixabay/Coverr (construction, cranes, ships, ports, factories), Openverse `arsiv` (named real structures; CC0/PD/CC BY only), NASA `nasa` (from space). Official developer renders of future projects are copyrighted: never use them; use our own graphics, CC images, or AI concept images labelled "Illustration" on screen (set `yapay_icerik_beyani: true` if realistic).

## Structure of a long video (8-12 min, ~1,300-1,800 words)
1. Hook (0-20 s): the impossible-sounding fact + the price tag.
2. Context (20-60 s): why it had to be built, open loop.
3. 3-5 chapters: the problem, the engineering trick, the build, the setbacks.
4. The bill: Cost Tower + from the buyer's desk.
5. Payoff: does it work / when will it be done.
6. Close (15 s): one-line takeaway + "which build should we cover next?"

## Red lines
No reused footage from other channels or manufacturers' videos, no copyrighted renders, no fake "finished" future projects, no investment advice, no politics/defamation (say "reported", cite), no real-person deepfakes.
