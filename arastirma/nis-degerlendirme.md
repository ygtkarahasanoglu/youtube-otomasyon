# Niche Evaluation — fully AI-produced, faceless, English

## Final decision (19 Oct 2026): Megaprojects + engineering, maritime-heavy — "Colossal Builds"

Chosen after scoring the owner's list of 100 faceless niches (details: Claude Docs "YouTube Kanal Kararı" → tab "100 Niş Değerlendirmesi"). 78 niches were dropped (copyrighted footage needed, politics/people, ad-restricted, AI-saturated, advice risk, needs filming). Of 8 finalist clusters, megaprojects / engineering / heavy machinery had the strongest new-channel evidence:

- NextGen Manufacturing — created 8 Nov 2025, 65 videos, 40.2M views, ~930K views in 14 days (Social Blade, 19 Oct 2026).
- The Factoran — 1.1 years, 282 videos, 31.4M views, estimated $14-20K/month ad revenue (OutlierKit estimate).
- MegaBuilds — created Oct 2023, 324 videos, 40.3M views, ~1.6M views in 14 days.
- A 3-year "mega builds" channel listed for sale at ~$2,044/month profit; audience mostly men 40-60 (Flippa, seller-reported).

Counter-evidence (survivorship bias): many new megaproject channels fail — e.g. Titan Megaprojects (17 videos, 52 subscribers), MegaStructure Vault (created May 2026, 15 videos, 20 subscribers), Curious Frontier (4 videos, 742 views). Hence the proven-demand rule in `kanal/kimlik.md`: no topic without prior proof of demand. Nothing guarantees views.

Maritime remains the owner's edge inside this niche (ships, shipyards, ports, canals, offshore), and every video keeps the cost angle (Cost Tower).

## Why the winner
- **Money:** business/finance-adjacent English audiences carry some of the highest published RPM ranges for faceless long-form (roughly $6–14 for business case studies per clipspeed/outlierkit compilations; treat as ranges, not promises).
- **Automation fit:** the visuals are naturally stock footage (factories, ports, stores), charts and AI illustrations — no face, no real footage of events needed. ElevenLabs narration is standard for the genre.
- **Policy fit:** YouTube's July 15, 2025 "inauthentic content" update targets mass-produced, templated, repetitive uploads. Original analysis (our own cost towers, sourced numbers, a distinct angle per video) plus a human approval step before publishing keeps us on the right side.
- **Edge:** the owner works in procurement — "how a price is really built" is a real expertise angle few faceless channels have.
- **Shorts:** each "real cost" breakdown produces strong 20–50 s Shorts (one number, one surprise).

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Inauthentic/repetitive content demonetization | Different structure and hook per video, no recycled stock clips (channel-wide registry), original graphics every video, human review before publishing |
| Wrong numbers damage trust | Every number sourced in `senaryo.json → kaynaklar`; estimates labeled on screen |
| Big competitors (Company Man, Wendover, ModernMBA…) | Narrower angle: price anatomy & procurement-insider view; mid-size companies and everyday products they skip |
| Stricter YPP entry from Feb 1, 2027 (8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views for new applicants) | Long-form first (watch hours), 2 Shorts per video only as discovery |
